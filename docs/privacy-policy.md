This Privacy Policy explains how InstGet ("we", "us", or "the App") collects, uses, shares and
protects information when you use the App. By using the App you agree to the practices described
here.

## Summary of key points

- **No account, no login.** We never ask for your name, email, phone number or social media
  credentials, unless you choose to contact us for support.
- **Your downloads stay on your device.** Media you save goes directly from the public source to
  your phone. We never receive, see or store the links, posts, usernames, captions or media you
  download.
- **We use a small set of service providers:** Google AdMob (ads in the free tier), Google
  Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (usage statistics and crash reports), Google Play
  Billing / Apple StoreKit (subscriptions) and Supabase (free-credit abuse prevention).
- **We do not sell your personal data.**
- The App is not intended for children under 13.
- Questions: **saanidhhyasoftwares@gmail.com**

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information we do NOT collect
- Social media login credentials, passwords, cookies of your accounts or session tokens.
- Profile data, follower lists or private messages.
- The links you paste or share into the App, or the content, usernames or captions of posts you
  download.
- Precise GPS location, contacts, photos or files from your device, biometric or other sensitive
  personal information.
- Payment card details. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we
  never receive card numbers or bank details.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

| What | Collected by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), device model, OS version, IP address (used to estimate country/region, not GPS), ad views and taps | Google AdMob | Showing ads in the free tier, measuring ads, preventing ad fraud |
| App usage events (for example: a download started, succeeded or failed and the media type; whether a rewarded ad was watched; whether the Premium screen was opened), an app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, approximate region derived from IP address, and subscription purchase events (product and price) | Google Analytics for Firebase | Understanding how the App is used and fixing broken features |
| Crash reports: stack traces, app state at the time of the crash, device model and OS version, a Crashlytics installation identifier | Firebase Crashlytics | Diagnosing and fixing crashes |
| A **one-way hashed device key** (a SHA-256 hash of the app-specific Android ID combined with an app secret) and the time it was first registered | Supabase (our backend) | Making sure the one-time free download credits are granted only once per device, so they cannot be reset by clearing app data or reinstalling |
| Subscription status (active/cancelled, product, purchase time) | Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit | Unlocking Premium features |

Notes:
- Google Analytics for Firebase is configured **not** to collect the Advertising ID.
- The hashed device key cannot be reversed to the Android ID, is specific to this App, and is
  never linked to your Advertising ID or used for advertising.
- Analytics events contain only categories (such as "Instagram" or "video"). They never contain
  links, usernames, captions or downloaded content.

### 1.3 Information you choose to give us
If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your
message.

## 2. How we use information
We use information only to:
- provide the App's features, including free download credits and Premium subscriptions;
- show ads to free-tier users (via Google AdMob);
- understand usage and fix bugs and crashes;
- prevent abuse of free credits and ad fraud;
- respond to support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant
effects on you.

## 3. Data sharing
- **Google AdMob** receives the data described in section 1.2 to serve and measure ads. Google acts
  under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics)** and **Supabase** process data on our behalf as
  service providers, only for the purposes above.
- **Google Play / Apple** process subscription payments under their own privacy policies.
- We may disclose information if required by law, court order or government authority, or as part
  of a merger or acquisition (subject to equivalent protections).

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

## 4. Data retention
- **Analytics data** is retained by Google Analytics for the retention period configured in our
  account, after which user-level data is deleted; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
- **Crash reports** are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
- **The hashed device key** is retained for as long as the free-credit feature is offered.
- **Support emails** are kept for up to 1 year after our last correspondence, then deleted.
- **Your downloads and in-app library** are stored only on your device and are removed when you
  delete them or uninstall the App.

## 5. Advertising and your choices
The free tier is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Premium subscribers see no ads.
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or opt out of ad personalisation.
- **iOS:** the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking in the App Tracking
  Transparency prompt; you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** you are shown Google's consent message when required and can choose
  how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice later by contacting us or
  reinstalling the App.

## 6. Security
All data the App sends (to AdMob, Firebase and Supabase) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no method of transmission or storage
is 100% secure. If a breach is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the relevant
authorities as required by law. Report suspected issues to saanidhhyasoftwares@gmail.com.

## 7. Children's privacy

live. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have, we will
