InstGet - save reels, videos and photos from IG and Threads links.

Save photos, videos, reels and carousels from public IG and Threads posts directly to your device - in original HD quality, with no login required.

Simply paste a post link into InstGet, or share the post to InstGet from the Share menu, and your media is saved to your gallery and your in-app Library for offline access anytime.

Features:

⬇️ Save photos, videos, reels and carousel posts (albums) from IG links
🎞️ Save Threads videos, images, carousel posts (albums) and text
🔍 Original HD quality - no extra compression
🚫 No login required - works with public posts
📤 Download straight from the Share menu
📂 In-app Library to browse and manage all your downloads
🖼️ Full-screen viewer with video playback and carousel swipe
📋 Copy post captions and hashtags
🔗 Open the original post from the Library
🌟 Premium plan for unlimited downloads and an ad-free experience

Good to know:

- Only public posts are supported. Private accounts and stories cannot be downloaded.
- Free downloads use credits; you can earn more by watching a short ad, or go Premium.
